## The 5 rules
1. Do it scared. You don't wait to feel ready.
2. The 10-minute rule. Stuck for 10 minutes? Ask, with a screenshot, what you tried and what you expected.
3. No AI until Week 4. Weeks 1–3 are by hand. (The help desk is not part of this rule: builders can ask it for help in any week.)
4. Explain every line. AI is allowed later; code nobody on your team can explain isn't.
5. Stay private. First names or handles only, the noreply email, no faces, phone numbers or keys in your code.
